# Experiment log and data dictionary

## Design recorded before the multi-seed runs

The primary activity outcome is the within-network difference between deviant and standard baseline-corrected L4 firing rates. The primary learning outcome is the deviant-minus-standard change in mean efficacy on anatomically present direct thalamus→L4 contacts, grouped by the presynaptic channel. The stage comparison is late minus early on each outcome. This design tests per-presentation response and cumulative change in efficacy separately. It does not assume that the rarer channel strengthens more. Fixed numerical parameters of the base circuit were piloted for nondegenerate operating activity before this experiment. See the model methods for those choices.

Each oddball block has 100 tokens separated by 300 ms, with 90 standards and 10 deviants. The time from tone onset to spike is measured in the half-open interval [0,100) ms. The local baseline uses [−100,0) ms. Subtract counts, divide by population size and 0.1 s, then average over presentations of that same role. Thus a standard's ninefold higher count cannot increase its **per-presentation** firing rate by simple accumulation. Shared schedules, underlying physical fiber identities and network realizations support paired comparisons. Two runs exchange which of channels 0 and 5 serves as the deviant. Replicates are the independent draws of both network and input, indexed by seed. Stimuli, neurons, and the two role assignments within a seed are repeated measurements. They do not independently contribute degrees of freedom to an interval.

| Conditions | Planned paired comparisons | Experimental question |
|---|---|---|
| `intact` in early and late stages | Eight seeds 1001–1008 in both physical assignments | Does the response or learning contrast change with the assumed stage parameter set? |
| `many`, `many_no_std` | Same eight seeds, both assignments, both stages | Compare the same physical rare target at matched onsets in oddball and an equiprobable ten-tone sequence. Repeat after removing depression. The control target has the same probability and timing, while neighboring tones, adaptation and network states can differ. |
| `no_stdp`, `no_std`, `no_plasticity` | Same eight seeds, both assignments, both stages | Disentangle fast synaptic depletion from accumulation of long-term direct weights. `no_plasticity` disables both mechanisms. |
| `no_sp_l4`, `no_inhibition`, `no_direct` | Same eight seeds, both assignments, both stages | Remove SP→L4 excitation, I→L4 inhibition, or direct thalamus→L4 transmission one at a time. SP→I remains active in the first manipulation. Direct efficacy at zero direct transmission is omitted from interpretation. |
| `threshold_lo/hi`, `recovery_lo/hi`, `rate_lo/hi`, `learning_lo/hi`, `width_lo/hi`, `ltp_lo/hi` | Four seeds 1001–1004 in both assignments and both stages | Change the firing threshold offset by −2/+2 mV, resource recovery time, stimulated input rate and connectivity width by factors 0.75/1.25, both STDP amplitudes by factors 0.5/1.5, or potentiation amplitude alone by factors 0.8/1.2 at fixed depression amplitude. All other parameters retain that stage's values. |
| `rate_8hz`, `sp_threshold_raised`, `l4_threshold_raised` | Four paired seeds | Revisit the sparse 8 Hz within-burst rate versus 50 Hz in both stages, and raise only the SP threshold in early or the L4 threshold in late by 2 mV. The original thresholds used different units. These are qualitative revisions of the notes, not numerical translations. |
| `late_sp_scale`, `late_direct_scale`, `late_tau_rec` | Starting from early, four seeds, both assignments | Change only one of the three stage-dependent parameters to its late value. These are individual interventions in the early background. Nonlinear interactions prevent attribution by simply adding their effects. |

First and second halves use 50 tokens each. Weight change at the end of either half is relative to the start of training. A difference between the two cumulative changes gives the incremental change during the second half. The response analysis in a half uses only events from that half. The history analysis compares a standard immediately following a deviant with a standard following another standard. Its value depends on sequence and underlying recovery dynamics. It is a descriptive check of repetition history.

A seed is the unit of uncertainty. Within a seed, first take the arithmetic mean of the two identity assignments. For ablations, developmental comparisons and comparisons between oddball and many tones, then difference measurements on the same seed. Report the across-seed mean and two-sided 95% Student-*t* interval for the mean. The number of degrees of freedom is the number of contributing independent seeds minus one. The intervals describe Monte Carlo uncertainty over these random circuits and inputs; they do not include model-parameter uncertainty, animal variability or adjustment for the exploratory comparisons. With four seeds, sensitivity intervals can be wide. Inspect individual points. A sign change across seeds, response windows, parameter values, or horizons weakens the corresponding generalization.

## Reading the tables

All files below live in `results/`. Full-field records are provided so the activity and learning results can be recomputed without digitizing plots.

| File | Row and conventions |
|---|---|
| `parameters.csv` | Regime and condition, exact configuration values and protocol. Negative/positive perturbation refers to the parameter's numerical value. |
| `token_counts.csv.gz` | One simulated token per condition, seed, regime and identity assignment. Presynaptic tone, role, onset (ms), raw SP/I/L4 population counts before and after onset. `std` and `dev` in a many-tone block are identity labels inherited from its corresponding oddball run. Every tone is equally probable in that control. |
| `run_metrics.csv` | Condition, regime, seed, swap (0 or 1), epoch. The `*_pre_hz`/`*_post_hz` columns give the local baseline and uncorrected evoked rate. `*_std_hz`/`*_dev_hz` are baseline-corrected. `*_mean_hz` is the unconditioned rate over that epoch's physical duration. `n_std` and `n_dev` count exposures. |
| `seed_metrics.csv` | The same measurements, averaged over both identity assignments within a seed. Averages of per-run statistics, including minima/maxima, refer to typical realization endpoints. Use `run_metrics.csv` for actual extrema. |
| `contrasts.csv` | Long-form intervals (`n, mean, lo, hi`). `level` summarizes an absolute measurement. `condition_minus_intact`, `late_minus_early`, `second_minus_first`, `oddball_minus_many`, and `std_after_deviant_minus_std_after_std` give the named paired difference in the metric's native units. |
| `weight_trajectories.csv` | Every five tokens for intact, no STD, no STDP and many-tone conditions, including the initial and final snapshot. `efficacy` is the dimensionless arithmetic mean of extant direct efficacies on fibers of the role's physical tone. Dimensionless efficacy and the gain that converts it into conductance are independent factors. |

The per-run response selectivity index is $(r_d-r_s)/(|r_d|+|r_s|)$, undefined if both baseline-corrected rates vanish. The absolute denominator keeps the index bounded even for a negative corrected response. The signed rate difference is the primary endpoint. Five-millisecond bins across the epoch quantify L4 synchrony. `l4_pair_r` is the arithmetic mean correlation of binned counts over distinct nonconstant L4-cell pairs. `l4_variable_fraction` reports the fraction of cells retained. `l4_peak_bin_fraction` is the largest fraction of the L4 population firing in any such bin. Shared tuning and time locking to tones can both raise raw correlations. The independent validation experiments also assess excess fine-timescale coincidence using a shuffled baseline. `weight_near_lower` and `weight_near_upper` are fractions of all connected direct efficacies within 5% of their bounds. The same fractions under different stages should be compared alongside the absolute maximum and the trajectory at longer exposure.

## Reproduction

From the repository root run `python run_experiments.py --jobs 4`. Adjust `--jobs` for local CPU limits. To check the pipeline with short blocks and a single seed, run `python run_experiments.py --quick --tokens 20 --output scratch/smoke`. A single seed has undefined uncertainty intervals. The schedule-integrity check compares every generated token against its companion condition and asserts matched rare positions/identities in both many-tone controls.

## Executed main matrix

The full run completed 536 simulations and 53,600 stimulation trials over eight independent primary seeds. It produced 1,608 run/epoch rows and 804 paired seed/epoch rows. Each of the 536 runs contained 100 trials, and every full oddball block contained exactly 90 standards and 10 deviants. The means below use Hz per L4 neuron, corrected for its population's local baseline. Paired intervals apply to contrasts, and should be read directly from the tables rather than inferred by subtracting independent marginal intervals.

| Full-block L4 measurement | Early | Late |
|---|---:|---:|
| Intact standard response, mean [95% interval] | 1.803 [1.700, 1.906] | 2.388 [2.257, 2.518] |
| Intact deviant response | 3.306 [2.916, 3.697] | 2.850 [2.635, 3.065] |
| Intact deviant minus standard response | 1.503 [1.158, 1.848] | 0.463 [0.305, 0.620] |
| Deviant minus standard with STDP disabled | 1.543 | 0.448 |
| Deviant minus standard with STD disabled | 0.281 [−0.501, 1.064] | 0.166 [−0.117, 0.449] |
| Deviant minus standard with both mechanisms disabled | 0.322 [−0.463, 1.107] | 0.072 [−0.217, 0.362] |
| Matched target, oddball minus many-tone response | 0.273 [0.157, 0.390] | 0.025 [−0.012, 0.062] |
| Matched target, same contrast with STD disabled | −0.002 [−0.005, 0.002] | 0.000 [−0.006, 0.006] |

The intact role contrast was positive in each seed in both stages. The paired late-minus-early difference was −1.041 [−1.279, −0.803] Hz. Removing STD reduced the contrast by 1.222 [0.606, 1.838] Hz early and 0.296 [0.046, 0.547] Hz late. Freezing STDP changed it by +0.040 [0.024, 0.056] Hz early and −0.015 [−0.048, 0.019] Hz late. On this time horizon the input and fast synaptic dynamics can generate the bulk of the activity contrasts with direct efficacies held fixed. Repetition history remains measurable among the standards themselves. The per-presentation rate after a deviant exceeded that after a standard by 0.983 [0.759, 1.207] Hz early and 0.280 [0.079, 0.481] Hz late. Rare target bursts in the many-tone control occur in a different frequency neighborhood; differential cross-frequency depression within this feedforward system suffices to produce a contextual difference.

**Pathway removals and unsuccessful conditions.** With SP→L4 excitation removed, early unconditional L4 firing fell from 0.679 to 0.00114 Hz, with deviant-minus-standard response of just 0.022 Hz. Late cells depended more strongly on the direct pathway. Removing it reduced unconditional L4 firing from 0.861 to 0.00135 Hz and left a 0.016 Hz contrast. A relative index can appear large in these nearly silent conditions, which is why the primary readout retains absolute units. Silencing I→L4 more than tripled late mean firing (0.861 to 3.154 Hz) and increased per-presentation deviant-minus-standard contrasts to 10.582 Hz early and 4.864 Hz late. The assumed inhibitory gain strongly regulates contrast magnitude.

**Developmental interventions.** Replacing only the early SP→L4 gain with its late value reduced the response contrast by 0.965 [0.555, 1.375] Hz. Replacing only the recovery time reduced it by 0.433 [0.139, 0.727] Hz. Replacing only the direct gain increased it by 0.250 [0.133, 0.368] Hz. The net response contrast therefore reflects competing parameter effects. At the nominal values SP's deviant-minus-standard contrast exceeded L4's by 1.733 [1.137, 2.329] Hz early and 1.800 [1.177, 2.422] Hz late. This SP advantage appeared in both regimes.

**Learning.** On direct contacts, early mean efficacy changed by +0.01394 [0.01061, 0.01727] on the standard channel and −0.00089 [−0.00283, 0.00105] on the deviant channel. The deviant-minus-standard change was −0.01483 [−0.01828, −0.01139], negative for all eight seeds. Early deviant-evoked activity therefore coexisted with stronger accumulated direct standard-channel learning. Late mean changes were −0.00271 [−0.00633, 0.00091] on the standard and −0.00215 [−0.00400, −0.00029] on the deviant. Their relative difference was +0.00057 [−0.00308, 0.00422]. The design assigns nine standard presentations per deviant. Total efficacy change measures learning over that exposure distribution. It cannot identify the plasticity caused by a single isolated tone presentation.

**Sensitivity.** Under the 12 moderate one-at-a-time low/high perturbations, mean L4 response contrasts remained positive in both regimes. Across these settings, means ranged from 1.409 to 1.923 Hz early and from 0.281 to 0.720 Hz late. The small late learning contrast was more fragile. At 37.5 vs 62.5 Hz input its means were −0.00853 [−0.01579, −0.00128] and +0.01023 [0.00160, 0.01887]. At −20% vs +20% LTP amplitude with unchanged LTD its means were +0.01015 [0.00244, 0.01786] and −0.00828 [−0.01667, 0.00011]. At the historically motivated 8 Hz rate, simulated responses were sparse and uncertainty on both role contrasts spanned zero, early 0.395 [−0.750, 1.540] Hz and late 0.251 [−0.869, 1.372] Hz. Raising only the early SP threshold by 2 mV gave a 1.681 Hz contrast; raising only the late L4 threshold by 2 mV gave 0.441 Hz. These interventions do not translate the old threshold units. They demonstrate how the original manual adjustments could alter the readout in a different model.

**Half-blocks and weight limits.** Second-minus-first half changes in the intact L4 activity contrast were −0.002 [−0.648, 0.645] Hz early and +0.028 [−0.549, 0.605] Hz late. Half-block estimates are noisy with about five rare presentations per half. The cumulative efficacy contrast diverged further in the early second half (increment −0.00595 [−0.00896, −0.00293]); the late increment was +0.00070 [−0.00254, 0.00394]. Across all intact individual runs, final extant direct efficacies ranged from 0.438 to 0.635 early and 0.427 to 0.569 late. No 100-token main experiment with direct transmission active ended with any direct weight within 5% of either bound. Raw mean pairwise L4 count correlations were 0.145 early and 0.116 late, with mean peak 5-ms coactivation of 30.6% and 29.5% of cells. Further coincidence controls and longer training horizons are documented separately.

## Independent cohort after the main experiment

The response and learning contrasts had different signs in the initial cohort and the late efficacy interval was close to zero. We consequently repeated the **unchanged** full-block analysis in 32 new seeds, 2001–2032, using both assignments and both stages, plus matched-target, no-STD, and frozen-STDP controls. The follow-up's purpose was to estimate sampling variability more precisely. It was selected after inspecting the first cohort. The estimates below keep the two cohorts separate. Run `python replicate_endpoints.py --jobs 4` for the 640 additional simulations. `replication_seeds.csv` retains paired values per seed. `replication_intervals.csv` contains their intervals. In addition to output spikes, the follow-up records the realized count of afferent events on the four matching fibers during each 50 ms burst, averaged by role within a run. The input difference was −0.011 [−0.377, 0.355] spikes per presentation. This check directly measures finite sequence and input imbalance.

| Independent cohort, 32 seeds; mean [95% interval] | Early | Late |
|---|---:|---:|
| Intact L4 deviant minus standard, Hz | +1.441 [1.299, 1.583] | +0.378 [0.289, 0.467] |
| Same physical deviant, oddball minus many, Hz | +0.252 [0.215, 0.289] | −0.010 [−0.033, 0.014] |
| Same matched target, STD disabled, Hz | −0.001 [−0.004, 0.002] | −0.002 [−0.006, 0.002] |
| Direct efficacy change, deviant minus standard | −0.01362 [−0.01487, −0.01237] | +0.00265 [0.00132, 0.00399] |

Early activity favored the deviant in 32/32 seed pairs and late activity in 31/32. The paired late-minus-early difference was −1.063 [−1.154, −0.972] Hz. All 32 early efficacy contrasts favored the standard. The small late relative change favored the deviant in 23/32; mean absolute change remained negative for **both** channels (standard −0.00475, deviant −0.00210). Thus a positive late learning contrast here corresponds to less mean depression of the deviant pathway. This direction is sensitive to the plasticity balance and input rate as shown above. With STD disabled, the raw oddball response contrasts were +0.051 [−0.198, 0.300] Hz early and +0.127 [0.007, 0.246] Hz late. Both corresponding matched-target context contrasts were near zero. Remaining raw differences can combine STDP, afferent realization, physical tuning and sequence effects. They cannot by themselves identify deviance detection.

The 32 independent intact networks were also repeated at four times the temporal resolution (`python replicate_endpoints.py --fine --jobs 4`, 128 additional runs). Fine-grid L4 response contrasts were +1.376 [1.240, 1.512] Hz early and +0.353 [0.266, 0.441] Hz late. Fine-grid relative efficacy changes were −0.01268 [−0.01390, −0.01146] early and +0.00334 [0.00203, 0.00465] late. The cohort-level directions therefore survived this refinement. Nominal and refined point estimates differed, which matters most near zero. The `replication_seeds_fine.csv` and `replication_intervals_fine.csv` tables retain the refined measurements. A separate refinement of the initial cohort covered the matched-target controls.
